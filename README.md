## 📖 About WM-DAgger

Imitation learning is a fundamental paradigm for transferring human expertise to robotic systems. However, its effectiveness is often hindered by **compounding errors** during real-world deployment. Even minor policy inaccuracies can drive the robot into out-of-distribution (OOD) states, leading to task failure.

To address this, we propose **WM-DAgger**, a novel framework that leverages generative **World Models (WMs)** to synthesize physically consistent recovery data. By simulating policy rollouts within a world model, we can actively explore OOD states and perform data aggregation autonomously—eliminating the need for costly human intervention.

### Core Modules
1. **Corrective Action Synthesis Module**: Synthesizes task-oriented recovery actions instead of random sub-optimal actions, ensuring the policy learns the correct behavior to recover from OOD states.
2. **Consistency-Guided Filtering Module**: Discards physical hallucinations generated by the world model by anchoring terminal frames to ground-truth expert demonstrations, ensuring the policy is trained on physically consistent data.
